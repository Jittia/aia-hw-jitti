Moat Momentum

Strengthening moats, found in the filings.

What it is

A research screener that ranks about 1,870 US companies on whether their competitive position is improving, using only audited SEC filings. Every number links to its 10-K; every weight and threshold can be changed; every official shortlist is frozen with a fingerprint.

What we have proved, and what we have not

  • Proved: picks keep above-industry quality. Three years later they ranked +8.3 points above their industry median, with ROIC 5.4% higher (2013–2023, point-in-time).
  • Not proved: early detection or excess returns. Only 26.9% of picks kept improving (universe 48.6%); the shortlist returned 14.3% a year vs 14.3% for the S&P 500 (2013–2026).

Why it is different

  • Free, public data: no licence cost.
  • Transparent rules, adjustable by the user, and a published record of where the method fails.
  • Founder: 30 years of global procurement and supply-chain leadership (P&G, Mars, Saint-Gobain, Danone, Tesco, Coca-Cola).

Business model

Subscription research tool and data/signal licensing to funds. Pricing and volumes are ASSUMPTIONS to test. Incumbents Morningstar, FactSet and MSCI together reported $8.1B of revenue in their latest 10-Ks.
