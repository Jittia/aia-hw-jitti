Moat MomentumProof · Methodology v1.0

Proof

Fundamental validation, 2013–2023, Methodology v1.0 locked before the run (settings fingerprint in prompts.md round 10). Each year the screen was re-run as of 30 June using only filings made by then, including companies that later delisted. Outcomes are read from later 10-Ks. No price data is used.

Main finding: the signal picks strong companies, but it does not yet predict further strengthening

  • Three years after being flagged, shortlist companies still rank +8.3 points above their industry median on returns, margins and turnover, and their clean ROIC is 5.4% higher. On the brief's test, they did have higher ROIC and peer rank later.
  • But they were already strong when flagged (median starting rank 81 of 100). Against companies that started at the same rank, their peer rank ended -3.4 points lower after 3 years; ROIC was 2.2% higher.
  • Only 26.9% of shortlist picks kept improving their position over 3 years, against 48.6% for everyone else; 38.0% deteriorated by more than 10 points, against 21.8%. Picking companies at the top of an improvement tends to catch the point where it slows down.
  • The result holds in every variant tried (weights ±10 points, level-heavy blend, stricter or no risk gate, 2 or 4 themes), in each sub-period and with energy or tech excluded. It is a property of the signal, not of one setting.

What this means for the product: the claim "a strengthening moat shows up in the numbers 1–3 years before it is recognised" is not supported by Methodology v1.0. The shortlist is a list of high-quality, improving businesses, and should not be described as predicting further improvement until a revised method passes a test on years it was not built on.
