Cleaned figures match the 10-K documents

10 companies from 10 different industries, 3 fiscal years each. Every input that feeds the clean-up and the themes was searched in the visible text of the 10-K it came from (hidden XBRL tags removed), in the units the filing prints. The clean figures were then recomputed by hand.

331 / 333non-zero inputs found in the 10-K text
60 / 60hand recalculations match the engine
17zero values (printed as a dash)
2weak matches (short number, no label nearby)

How the 10 companies were chosen

Rule set before looking at results: the highest-ranked company in each of the 10 largest SIC divisions of the 2026-11-03 run. Five are on the shortlist (SEZL, HXL, COCO, FSS, SMTI).

What the check found and changed

  • Operating income tag can be a segment total. TechnipFMC's income statement has no operating-income line: revenue $9,083.3M − total costs $8,126.5M = $956.8M (FY2024), while its OperatingIncomeLoss tag is $1,157.3M. Across the universe, 48 of 1,870 companies show a gap above 10% of operating income (and above 1% of revenue) between the tag and revenue − total costs. Their data confidence is now one level lower, with a Risk observation. The value itself is kept.
  • Gains on selling a business sit below operating income. TechnipFMC's $71.3M gain on disposal of Measurement Solutions is printed below total costs. Such gains (328 companies in the latest year) are no longer subtracted; they are shown in the Deep-Dive bridge as "not removed". Gains on selling equipment and other assets are still removed.
  • After both fixes the shortlist is unchanged (same 25 names).

Limits

  • Finding a number in the text shows the company printed it; it does not prove it sits on the line we think. Line labels are checked near short numbers only.
  • Whether each one-off is inside operating income is not visible in XBRL facts; this was checked by hand for TechnipFMC only.
  • Weak matches: MU FY2025 Restructuring, GSAT FY2023 Long-lived asset impairment.
