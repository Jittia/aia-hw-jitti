Does the Layer 1 score work? Backtest

The score was run as of a past date using only filings public at that time, then compared with the stocks that went on to return ≥10x over the next 5 years.

10-bagger rate by market cap at the start

Weight variants tested

Survivorship bias: only companies still listed today are included, so hit rates are optimistic. Small sample: ~25 ten-baggers per window, so 1–2 hits between variants is noise. Weights were chosen by looking at both windows; the honest next test is tracking this watchlist forward.

Method and sources

  • Numbers: SEC EDGAR XBRL companyfacts (data.sec.gov), quarters derived point-in-time; TTM revenue matches an independent screener within 1% for 96.5% of companies.
  • Score (Layer 1): ; penalties for >25% dilution, lumpy revenue, acquisition-driven growth and 10x outcomes above $1T.
  • Catalyst review (Layer 2): AI readers restricted to the filing text; every quote and URL verified by code.
  • Prices / market cap: Yahoo Finance close × SEC cover-page shares.
  • Code, logs and the full backtest report: this repository (src/, prompts.md, output/backtest_report.md).
