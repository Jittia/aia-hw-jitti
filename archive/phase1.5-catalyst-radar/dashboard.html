Catalyst-of-Change Radar · US

Every quarter, each US company's SEC filings are checked for a change: revenue accelerating, gross margin stepping up, turning profitable, contracted revenue jumping. A company that changes faster than its industry, grows organically and still has room to 5x becomes Emerging; two quarters in a row makes it Confirmed. AI then reads its filings to confirm the catalyst. Research watchlist, not investment advice.

Does it work? Event study 2012 → 2021

Every signal and stage entry since 2012, dated by its SEC filing, followed for 5 years. Lift = how much more often it reached ≥5x than all companies from the same months (1.0 = random). Rules were set on ; the holdout () was not used to choose anything.

Survivorship bias: only companies listed today are included, so hit rates are optimistic; lifts compare like with like. Radar alone shows no holdout edge: the edge comes from the Emerging filters.

Method and sources

  • Changes: SEC EDGAR XBRL (first-reported values, dated by filing). Radar triggers: . Insider cluster buys (SEC Form 4 data sets) and 8-K items are shown as context only: they had no edge in the tuning period.
  • Emerging filters: TTM growth ≥ industry median + ; organic (acquisition flags off); dilution ≤ ; revenue growth needed for 5x ≤ /yr at a sector sales multiple. 10x stretch = the same test passes for 10x.
  • AI review: readers restricted to the company's own filing text; every quote and link checked by code.
  • Prices / market cap: Yahoo Finance close × SEC share count. Code, logs and the full event-study report are in this repository (src/, prompts.md, output/event_study_report.md).
